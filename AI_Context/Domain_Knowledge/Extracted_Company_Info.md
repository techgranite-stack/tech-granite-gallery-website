## 🔨 2. Surface Finishes Specification

Handwritten records list 8 proprietary mechanical/chemical stone treatment processes:

| Finish Name | Target Texture | Best For Stones | Maintenance | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mirror Polished** | Glossy & Reflective | Marble, Onyx, Granite | Medium | Progressive diamond-pad buffing that opens and locks the natural crystalline depth. |
| **Leathered** | Supple Matte / Undulating | Granite, Quartzite, Marble | Low | Diamond-tipped brushes sweep at high velocity to produce a satin touch. |
| **Flamed** | Coarse & Anti-slip | Granite, Sandstone | Low | Oxyacetylene flame bursting crystals to create maximum slip-resistance. |
| **Shot Blast** | Uniform Matte / Fine-pocked | Granite, Limestone | Low | Blasting steel micro-balls centrifugally for high-traffic paving. |
| **Bush Hammered** | Rugged Pitted / Weathered | Limestone, Sandstone | Medium | Multi-point steel hammers striking repeatedly to emulate aged masonry. |
| **Chiseled / Rock Face**| Primal Fractured 3D | Granite, Sandstone | Medium | Hand-splitting along natural cleavage planes for bold geological relief. |
| **Acid Wash** | Antique Etched Sheen | Marble, Limestone | Medium | Dilute chemical wash to create an antiqued Tuscan/Rajput palace floor effect. |
| **Hydro Blast** | Organic Crystalline Grip | Granite, Quartzite | Low | Ultra-high-pressure clean water jets stripping outer layers chemically-free. |

## 📐 4. High-Yield Application Areas

Standard classification rules for natural stone allocations:

1. **Flooring**: Large-format gang-saw cut slabs (thickness 16mm/18mm) with low grout lines to maximize mirror reflections.
2. **Kitchen Countertops**: High-density granites and quartzites (e.g. Absolute Black) to resist etches from lemon juice and food acids.
3. **Bathroom Vanities & Cladding**: Soft white marbles (Volakas, Carrara) paired with antique or honed finishes to reduce slippage and fingerprints.
4. **Wall Cladding & Backlit Panels**: Translucent onyx block slices (Jade Green, Honey) illuminated with structural cool/warm LED arrays.
5. **Exterior & Landscaping**: Weatherproof flamed granites, sandstones, and shot-blasted limestones to resist monsoons and summer heat indefinitely.
